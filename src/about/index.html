We love our work. Clients notice.

{{brand.name}} is a building automation and control-systems integrator in South Florida. We design, install, program and service every system in the building, and we stay on the account after hand-over.

Where we come from

{{brand.name}} was founded by Brayan Turnquest, an automation engineer with clear knowledge and clear goals: to meet the needs of his customers with the best service in automation and control, providing the advice, the technology and the quality in installation and maintenance that a building deserves. His challenge, and his team's, is a service company where total commitment to the client, alliances and agility in every process make it a reference in the automation sector.

His work spans the full range of what a building can ask of its controls: direct digital control of HVAC and mechanical plant, supervisory systems, custom control panels and the people who run them once the integrator has left.

Before South Florida, that work was done in the Bahamas under the Caiman Automation name. The projects below are from that period and are the reason the company exists: each one taught us something about how systems fail when they are treated as separate contracts, and how they behave when one team owns all of them.

Experience highlights

Three projects that shaped how we work.

  • Central Bank of The Bahamas, new headquarters

    Consultant on the facility master system for the new building: control and supervision of the mechanical systems using direct digital controls, applied to HVAC and non-HVAC systems alike.

  • Lynden Pindling International Airport, Nassau

    Responsible for the airport's building management system with the Nassau Airport Development Company for more than three years: maintenance, upgrades and day-to-day operation of a system that cannot stop.

  • Hotel Iberostar Grand Packard

    One of the engineers who carried the hotel automation project through every stage, from the design and assembly of the control panels to the supervisory system that runs the property.

  • Homes, buildings and industry

    New system installations, troubleshooting and optimisation of existing equipment, and preventive maintenance across home, building and industrial automation, including PLC, drive and HMI panel design and assembly.

Mission and vision

  • Mission

    Cover the needs of our customers and offer the best service in automation and control, providing the advice, technology and quality that installations and maintenance require.

  • Vision

    To be a service company where total commitment to the client, alliances and agility in our processes make us a benchmark in our sector, growing and diversifying each of our services across South Florida.

Finding a permanent solution is our ultimate goal.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Total commitment to the client

    Advice first, then technology. We recommend what the building needs, on platforms you are free to leave.

  • One team for every system

    HVAC, lighting, shades, access, video, energy and air quality from the same engineers, so nothing falls between trades.

  • Agility

    Small enough that the engineer who designed the system answers the phone when it alarms.

  • A permanent solution

    We would rather fix the sequence than reset the alarm. The job is done when the building stays fixed.
