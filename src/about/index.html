We love our work. Clients notice.

A building automation and control-systems integrator in South Florida. We design, install, program and service every system in the building, and we stay on the account after hand-over.

Where we come from

{{brand.name}} was founded by Brayan Turnquest, an automation engineer whose work covers everything a building can ask of its controls: direct digital control of HVAC and mechanical plant, supervisory systems, custom control panels, and the people who run them once the integrator has left.

Before South Florida, that work was done in the Bahamas under the Caiman Automation name. The projects below are from that period, and they are the reason this company runs the way it does: one team owning every system, so nothing falls between contracts.

Experience highlights

  • Central Bank of The Bahamas

    Consultant on the facility master system for the new headquarters: control and supervision of the mechanical systems with direct digital controls, on HVAC and non-HVAC systems alike.

  • Lynden Pindling International Airport, Nassau

    Responsible for the airport's building management system for more than three years with the Nassau Airport Development Company: maintenance, upgrades and daily operation of a system that cannot stop.

    Every stage of the hotel automation project, from the design and assembly of the control panels to the supervisory system that runs the property.

Plus new installations, troubleshooting and preventive maintenance across home, building and industrial automation, including PLC, drive and HMI panel design and assembly.

Mission

Cover the needs of our customers and offer the best service in automation and control, with the advice, technology and quality that installations and maintenance require.

Vision

A service company where total commitment to the client, alliances and agility in our processes make us a benchmark in our sector, growing and diversifying each of our services across South Florida.

Finding a permanent solution is our ultimate goal.
