Buildings we work in.

The same team and the same open platforms, sized to the property. Pick a building type to see the rooms we work in and what goes into them.

Residential

Large homes and estates where lighting, climate, shades, security and media behave as one system. As the afternoon sun reaches the glass, the shades lower on their own; one keypad press by the door sets the whole house to "away".

  • Home automation (Crestron)
  • Lighting and shading (Lutron)
  • Access control, cameras and intercom
  • Service and support

Condominiums and HOA

Common areas, chiller plant, garages, pool equipment, access and utilities managed from one interface, with sub-metering that bills each unit for what it uses. Boards get reports they can read; management gets alarms before residents call.

  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Access control and video
  • Energy monitoring and sub-metering
  • Indoor air quality and garage exhaust

Commercial offices

Open floors with automated shades on the sun side, lighting to code, conference rooms that switch on from one panel, after-hours overrides billed to the tenant, and one interface for the property team. Open protocols keep the next fit-out competitive.

  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Lighting and shading (Lutron)
  • Conference rooms (Crestron)
  • Tenant sub-metering

Hospitality

Guest rooms that set themselves for arrival, public areas with shading and lighting scenes by time of day, and back-of-house plant, kitchens and laundry on the same platform with energy reporting for the owner.

  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Lighting and shading (Lutron)
  • Guest-facing automation (Crestron)
  • Energy monitoring

Healthcare

Air quality, pressure relationships between rooms and access to controlled areas, logged to the standards your compliance depends on. The BMS holds the records; the access system holds the audit trail.

  • Indoor air quality
  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Access control and video
  • Service and support

Retail

Lighting scenes, storefront shades and security tuned to opening hours, and climate that follows the schedule rather than the thermostat someone touched last week.

  • Lighting and shading (Lutron)
  • Access control and video
  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)

Light industrial

Process spaces, ventilation, motor control and energy tracked in one place. We design and assemble control panels, including PLC, variable-frequency-drive and remote I/O panels, and supervise them from the same platform as the rest of the building.

  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Energy monitoring and analytics
  • Indoor air quality
  • Control panels, service and support
