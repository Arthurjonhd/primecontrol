Residential

Large homes and estates.

Lighting, climate, shades, security, cameras, intercom, gates, irrigation and media, behaving as one system from a keypad, a touch panel or a phone. The interface is Crestron; the lighting and shades are Lutron; the rest is chosen for the house.

  • Home and commercial automation (Crestron)
  • Lighting and shading control (Lutron)
  • Access control and video
  • Service and support

Condominiums and HOA

Towers, mid-rise and gated communities.

Common areas, chiller plant, garages, pool equipment, access and utilities managed from one interface, with sub-metering that bills each unit for what it uses. Boards get reports they can read; management gets alarms before residents call.

  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Access control and video
  • Energy monitoring and analytics
  • Indoor air quality

Commercial offices

Single-tenant and multi-tenant buildings.

After-hours tenant overrides with billing, sub-metering by suite, lighting to code, conference rooms that run themselves and one interface for the property team. Open protocols keep the next chiller or the next tenant fit-out competitive.

  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Energy monitoring and analytics
  • Lighting and shading control (Lutron)
  • Conference rooms and boardrooms (Crestron)

Hospitality

Hotels, resorts and clubs.

Guest comfort and back-of-house efficiency from the same controls: room climate and lighting scenes, public-area shading, kitchen and laundry ventilation, chiller plant and energy reporting, with cameras and access on the same platform.

  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Lighting and shading control (Lutron)
  • Guest-facing automation (Crestron)
  • Energy monitoring and analytics

Healthcare

Clinics, surgical centres and medical offices.

Air quality, pressure relationships between rooms and access to controlled areas, logged to the standards your compliance depends on. The BMS holds the records; the access system holds the audit trail.

  • Indoor air quality
  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Access control and video
  • Service and support

Retail

Storefronts, showrooms and centres.

Lighting scenes, storefront shades and security tuned to opening hours, and climate that follows the schedule rather than the thermostat someone touched last week.

  • Lighting and shading control (Lutron)
  • Access control and video
  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)

Light industrial

Warehouses, workshops and process spaces.

Process spaces, ventilation, motor control and energy tracked in one place. We design and assemble control panels, including PLC, variable-frequency-drive and remote I/O panels, and supervise them from the same platform as the rest of the building.

  • Building automation (BMS / BAS)
  • Energy monitoring and analytics
  • Indoor air quality
  • Control panels, service and support
